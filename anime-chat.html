Chat tips

Behtar jawab lene ke liye yeh try karo:

With characters

  • Unse advice maango: "Give me some advice".
  • Unki power ke baare mein poochho: "How strong are you?".
  • Apna mood batao, woh apne style mein jawab dein ge.

With anime guides

  • Poochho: "What is the story about?" ya "Should I watch it?".
  • Genres aur rating card par hi dikh jati hai.
  • Naya anime dhoondne ke liye genre chips use karo.

Frequently asked questions

Kya yeh chat free hai?

Haan, bilkul free hai. Koi login ya payment nahi chahiye.

Characters kaise jawab dete hain?

Har character ke pehle se likhe hue jawab hain jo tumhare sawal ke keywords (jese advice, power, sad) se match hote hain.

Anime list load kyun nahi hoti?

Anime data AniList se aata hai, to internet chahiye. Connection check karke page refresh karo.

Apna naya character kaise add karun?

js/chat.js mein CHARS list ka koi block copy karo aur naam, rang, emoji aur replies badal do.

Dark ya Light mode kaise lagaun?

Header mein sun/moon wala toggle dabao. Tumhari pasand yaad rakhi jati hai.
