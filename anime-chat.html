Chat tips

Try these for better answers:

With characters

  • Ask for advice: "Give me some advice".
  • Ask about their power: "How strong are you?".
  • Share your mood and they'll answer in their own style.

With anime guides

  • Ask: "What is the story about?" or "Should I watch it?".
  • Genres and rating are shown right on the card.
  • Use the genre chips to discover a new anime.

Frequently asked questions

Is this chat free?

Yes, it's completely free. No login or payment needed.

How do the characters reply?

Each character has pre-written replies that match keywords in your message (like advice, power or sad).

Why isn't the anime list loading?

The anime data comes from AniList, so you need an internet connection. Check your connection and refresh the page.

How do I add my own character?

Open js/chat.js, copy any block in the CHARS list and change the name, colors, emoji and replies.

How do I switch dark or light mode?

Click the sun/moon toggle in the header. Your choice is remembered.
