Death Note: The Psychological Masterpiece

Death Note ki kahani shuru hoti hai Light Yagami naam ke ek behad intelligent high school student se, jo apni zandgi se bore ho chuka hai aur duniya ke andar faili hui crime aur corruption ko dekh kar pareshan hai. Ek din uske paas achanak ek mysterious black notebook girti hai, jiska naam "Death Note" hota hai. Is notebook ko Ryuk naam ke ek Shinigami (god of death) ne insani duniya mein phenka hota hai. Death Note ka rule yeh hai ki agar aap kisi ka naam isme likhte waqt uska chehra yaad rakhenge, toh us insaan ki maut ho jayegi.

Is taaqat ko milne ke baad Light Yagami duniya ke sabhi criminals ko khatam karke ek nayi aur saaf-suthri duniya ka bhagwan banne ki thaan leta hai aur "Kira" ke naam se mashhoor ho jata hai. Jab duniya bhar mein bina kisi wazah ke criminals maut ke munh mein jane lagte hain, toh international police aur Interpol bebas ho jate hain. Tab entry hoti hai duniya ke sabse rahasyamayi aur brilliant detective ki, jise duniya "L" ke naam se jaanti hai.

L aur Light ke beech ek khatarnak aur romanchak (thriller) cat-and-mouse game shuru hota hai. L ko pata chal jata hai ki Kira Japan ke Kanto region mein hai aur woh Light ko hi sabse bada suspect man kar uske peeche pad jata hai. Dono ke beech dimaagi jang chhid jati hai jismein Misa Amane (jisne doosri Death Note milti hai) aur kai doosre characters aate hain. Yeh poori kahani justice, morality aur power ke misuse par adharit hai.

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba — The Epic Journey

Demon Slayer ki kahani Taisho-era Japan ki hai, jahan Tanjiro Kamado naam ka ek saadhu aur mehnati ladka apne parivar ka khyal rakhne ke liye pahad se koyla bechne shehar jata hai. Ek din jab woh ghar wapas lautta hai, toh dekhta hai ki ek khunkhaar demon ne uske poore parivar ko qatal kar diya hai. Kewal uski choti behan, Nezuko Kamado, zinda bachti hai, lekin demon ke hamle ki wajah se woh khud ek khatarnak demon mein badal chuki hoti hai.

Halanki Nezuko demon ban chuki hai, par woh abhi bhi apne bhai Tanjiro ko pehchanti hai aur uspar hamla nahi karti. Tabhi wahan Giyu Tomioka naam ka ek Demon Slayer aata hai jo Nezuko ko khatam karna chahta hai, lekin Tanjiro ke anurodh aur Nezuko ki insaniiyat ko dekh kar woh unhe ek rasta batata hai. Tanjiro apni behan ko wapas insaan banane aur apne parivar ka badla lene ke liye Demon Slayer Corps mein shamil hone ka faisla karta hai.

Tanjiro sakht training ke baad Water Breathing technique seekhta hai aur apni yatra ke dauran Zenitsu Agatsuma aur Inosuke Hashibira jaise doston se milta hai. Yeh sabhi mil kar Muzan Kibutsuji (Demon King) aur uske powerful demons (Twelve Kizuki) ke khilaf ladai ladte hain. Yeh kahani bhai-behan ke pyaar, himmat aur dukh ke samundar mein umeed ki kiran ko darshati hai.

Hell's Paradise: Jigokuraku — Dark Mystery Story

Hell's Paradise ki kahani Edo period ke dauran ki hai, jahan Gabimaru the Hollow naam ka ek sabse khatarnak aur nirdai ninja assassin rehta hai jo apne gaon ki taraf se dhokha khane ke baad mout ki saza ka samna kar raha hota hai. Gabimaru ke upar kai tarike ke execution (faansi, aag, aur crushing) azmaye jate hain, lekin uski body par kisi cheez ka asar nahi hota kyunki uske andar apni patni se milne ki zinda rehne ki zidd hoti hai.

Tab wahan Yamada Asaemon Sagiri naam ki ek shatir executioner aati hai aur use ek shart rakhti hai ki agar woh ek rahasyamayi aur khatarnak island par ja kar wahan se immortal life ka elixir (amrit) dhoond kar layega, toh shogun use maaf kar dega aur use aazadi milegi. Gabimaru is mission ke liye tayar ho jata hai aur baaki death-row criminals ke sath us island ki taraf rawana hota hai.

Woh island koi aam jagah nahi balkay ek bhayanak nark jaisi jannat hoti hai jahan ajeeb-o-gareeb monstrous deities aur jaanleva paudhe hote hain. Is mission ke dauran Gabimaru aur Sagiri ko survival ki ek anokhi jang ladni padti hai jismein unhe apni taaqat aur vishwas dono ka imtihan dena padta hai.
