Story 01

Death Note: The Psychological Masterpiece

  • Psychological Thriller
  • Studio: Madhouse
  • Year: 2006

Light Yagami is a brilliant high school student who is bored with his life and troubled by the crime and corruption spreading across the world. One day a mysterious black notebook falls into his hands. It is called the "Death Note", and it was dropped into the human world by a Shinigami (god of death) named Ryuk. The rule is simple and terrifying: anyone whose name is written in the notebook while the writer pictures their face will die.

With this power, Light decides to wipe out the world's criminals and become the god of a new, clean world. People soon begin calling him "Kira". As criminals die without explanation all over the globe, the international police and Interpol find themselves helpless. Then the world's most mysterious and brilliant detective steps forward, known only as "L".

A dangerous and thrilling cat-and-mouse game begins between L and Light. L works out that Kira is operating in Japan's Kanto region and soon treats Light as his prime suspect. A battle of minds follows, with Misa Amane, who holds a second Death Note, and many other characters pulled into the conflict. At its heart, the story is about justice, morality, and the misuse of power.

Light Yagami L Ryuk Misa Amane
Story 02

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba — The Epic Journey

  • Dark Fantasy & Action
  • Studio: ufotable
  • Year: 2019

Demon Slayer is set in Taisho-era Japan, where Tanjiro Kamado, a kind and hardworking boy, travels down from the mountains to sell charcoal and support his family. When he returns home, he finds that a ruthless demon has slaughtered his entire family. Only his younger sister, Nezuko, is still alive, but the attack has turned her into a dangerous demon herself.

Even as a demon, Nezuko still recognizes her brother and does not attack him. A Demon Slayer named Giyu Tomioka arrives intending to put her down, but Tanjiro's pleading and Nezuko's lingering humanity change his mind, and he points them toward a path forward. Determined to turn his sister back into a human and avenge his family, Tanjiro decides to join the Demon Slayer Corps.

After brutal training, Tanjiro masters the Water Breathing technique and meets companions like Zenitsu Agatsuma and Inosuke Hashibira along his journey. Together they face Muzan Kibutsuji, the Demon King, and his powerful Twelve Kizuki. It is a story of brotherly love, courage, and a spark of hope in an ocean of sorrow.

Tanjiro Kamado Nezuko Kamado Zenitsu Agatsuma Inosuke Hashibira
Story 03

Hell's Paradise: Jigokuraku — Dark Mystery Story

  • Supernatural & Mystery
  • Studio: MAPPA
  • Year: 2023

Hell's Paradise takes place in the Edo period and follows Gabimaru the Hollow, one of the most dangerous and ruthless ninja assassins ever known. Betrayed by his own village, he now sits on death row. Executioners try one method after another, including hanging, fire, and crushing, but nothing can kill him, because his stubborn will to live is driven by his wish to see his wife again.

Then Yamada Asaemon Sagiri, a sharp and calculating executioner, offers him a deal. If Gabimaru travels to a mysterious and perilous island and brings back the elixir of immortal life, the Shogun will pardon him and set him free. Gabimaru accepts the mission and sets sail for the island with a group of other death-row criminals.

The island is no ordinary place. It is a paradise that feels like hell, filled with strange, monstrous deities and deadly plants. Throughout the mission, Gabimaru and Sagiri must fight a strange and desperate battle for survival that tests both their strength and their faith.

Gabimaru Yamada Asaemon Sagiri
